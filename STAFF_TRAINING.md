# MediNova Staff Training

## Reception

1. Verify the patient phone and preferred language (English/Bengali).
2. Register or select the correct patient profile; never create duplicate patients.
3. Confirm branch, doctor, date, and slot before collecting payment.
4. Record cash as **Pay at Counter** and issue the receipt number.
5. Check in with the QR scanner; the patient receives a queue number.
6. Call the next patient only from the queue board and confirm their name aloud.
7. Reschedule or cancel only within the configured cancellation window.

## Doctors

1. Review the daily timeline and patient summary before starting.
2. Confirm allergies, blood group, medications, and AI triage notes.
3. Call the next patient and start the consultation.
4. Record diagnosis, medicines, dosage/frequency/duration, advice, and follow-up date.
5. Generate the prescription PDF and confirm it appears in the patient portal.
6. Mark completed or no-show accurately; never share another patient’s records.
